# Source and authored changes

Source: Google Research Taskmaster-1, conversation
`dlg-00055f4e-4a46-48bf-8d99-4e477663eb23`, instruction `restaurant-table-2`,
20 utterances. Human-created research dialogue, not a verified commercial call.

The original source, its CC BY 4.0 notice, attribution and hashes are preserved
unchanged in `baseline/source/` and `baseline/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
[Source repository](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Taskmaster)
[Dataset notice](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Taskmaster/blob/master/TM-1-2019/README.md)
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The separately authored original adaptation package was unavailable. The baseline
was reconstructed from the user-supplied request and handoff; it is preserved
byte-for-byte here. Its fictional Mumbai branches, shared central desk, fixed
date, regular single table and five-option inventory are authored adaptations.

The three new variants were authored for the user's approved harder-case plan.
Added material includes Hinglish briefs, declared spoken mistakes, event triggers,
quote and counteroffer policy, the 13,200/12,000 rupee fictional dining totals,
two guests without both onion and garlic, a birthday question, confirmation
revisions and additional grading rules. None is original Taskmaster dialogue or
availability. All business tools are mock actions. No generated conversation or
live result is included. Synthetic tests are harness validation, not datapoints.

The methodology is informed by the cited tau-bench evaluation and voice documents
in GUIDE.md; no tau-bench task data or source code was copied into this package.
